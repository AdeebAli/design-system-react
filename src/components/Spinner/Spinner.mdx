# Spinner

A short wait with no measure of progress.

## Usage

Use `Spinner` when something is working and its duration is unknown. Use [`Skeleton`](?path=/docs/components-data-skeleton--docs) when the content's shape is known, and [`Progress`](?path=/docs/components-feedback-and-display-progress--docs) when the amount done is known. A [`Button`](?path=/docs/components-forms-and-actions-button--docs) has its own `loading` state.

- It draws in `currentColor`.
- With reduced motion it stops turning and stays visible.
- It works in server components.

## Do's and don'ts

<DoDont>
  <Do>
    Give it a `label` when nothing nearby says what's happening.
    <Canvas of={Stories_.Labeled} />
  </Do>
  <Dont
    code={`<Spinner />
// Alone in an empty panel, hidden from screen readers`}
  />
</DoDont>

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use `Button`'s `loading` for a button that's working.</Do>
  <Dont
    code={`<Button disabled>
  <Spinner size="sm" /> Saving
</Button>`}
  />
</DoDont>

## Accessibility

- Without `label` it's hidden from screen readers, for when nearby text or `aria-busy` already says what's happening.
- With `label` it's a `role="status"`, but a status that mounts with its text isn't always announced, so keep a status region mounted when the announcement matters.
