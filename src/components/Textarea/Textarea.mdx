# Textarea

A multi-line text input, at least two rows tall.

## Usage

Use `Textarea` for longer text, such as a comment or a description. For one line, use [`Input`](?path=/docs/components-forms-and-actions-input--docs). Label it with a [`Field`](?path=/docs/components-forms-and-actions-field--docs).

- It keeps the native `value` and `onChange`, and works with `register`.
- `ref` and every other prop go to the `textarea`.
- `resize` sets whether people can resize it. It's `vertical` by default.
- It's a client component.

## Accessibility

- It renders a native `textarea`. In a `Field`, it gets the label, hint, error, and `aria-invalid`.
