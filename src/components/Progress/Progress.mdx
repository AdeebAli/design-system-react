# Progress

How far along something is.

## Usage

Use `Progress` when there's a measure, such as an upload's percentage. Use [`Spinner`](?path=/docs/components-feedback-and-display-spinner--docs) when there's no measure, and [`Skeleton`](?path=/docs/components-data-skeleton--docs) when the content's shape is known.

- `value={null}` is indeterminate: a segment slides across the track, and with reduced motion it's a static partial fill.
- It's for progress, not scores such as storage used.
- It's a client component.

## Do's and don'ts

<DoDont>
  <Do>
    Give it a visible label.
    <Canvas of={Stories_.Playground} />
  </Do>
  <Dont
    code={`<Progress value={40} />
// No label or aria-label`}
  />
</DoDont>

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use text for a measure that isn't progress, such as storage used.</Do>
  <Dont code={`<Progress value={82} label="Storage used" />`} />
</DoDont>

## Accessibility

- It has the `progressbar` role with its value. It needs a visible `label` or an `aria-label`.
